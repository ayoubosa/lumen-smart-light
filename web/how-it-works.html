How it works

A small board on your Wi-Fi controls the lights. You use this website to tell it what to do, and a motion sensor can do it for you.

What happens when you walk in

  1. Sense

    The motion sensor notices you. The light sensor checks whether it is dark.

  2. Decide

    The ESP32 board switches on the rooms you picked under Motion lighting.

  3. Show

    Every phone or laptop with this website open updates at the same moment.

The parts

  • ESP32 board

    The brain. It joins your Wi-Fi, serves this website, and switches the lights. Find it at lumen.local on your network.

  • Motion sensor (PIR)

    Detects body heat moving in front of it, up to about 7 metres away.

  • Dimmable LED

    The living-room light. The board dims it smoothly from 0 to 100%.

  • Relay module

    Two switches for the bedroom and kitchen lights. Relays are on or off, with no dimming.

The prototype

The system was built on a model house first, then tested in several rooms at the university with a dimmable LED, the motion sensor and the light sensor.
